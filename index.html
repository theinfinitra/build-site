Common Questions

Before you apply.

Honest answers to the things people ask most.

How much does it cost?

₹60,000, paid upfront at the start of the program. You earn ₹20,000 back during the paid internship (₹10,000/month × 2 months). Net cost: ₹40,000. For context: a shared PG room near a tech hub in Hyderabad or Bangalore costs ₹8-10K/month — that's ₹48-60K for just a room for 6 months. Here, that same amount gets you mentors, office space, live projects, cloud infrastructure, and a direct path to employment. And you stay home.

Do I need a laptop?

Yes. You'll need your own laptop for the program.

Do I need coding experience?

Basic coding familiarity is strongly recommended — even a bit of Python or HTML from YouTube counts. You don't need to be an expert, but you shouldn't be starting from absolute zero.

What are the timings?

Monday to Saturday, 6 hours a day. This is a full-time commitment — you can't do this alongside another course or job.

I'm from a nearby town, not Kurnool. Can I join?

Yes. Kurnool is affordable to live in — PG or hostel for ₹3,000–5,000/month. Some builders relocate from nearby districts.

Is the job guaranteed?

No. Top performers get hired at Infinitra. Everyone who completes the internship gets a formal experience letter — plus 8+ shipped projects, AWS cert prep, and real work experience that gets interviews elsewhere.

What's the selection process?

Apply online → take the online challenge (~20 minutes, 4 questions — you can use any tool including AI) → shortlisted candidates come for a half-day at our studio. We look at how you solve problems, not what you already know.

What's the online challenge like?

4 short questions (~5 min each): research something real, solve a practical problem, make a judgment call, and use an AI tool. No trick questions, no coding required. We're looking at how you think, not what you already know. Use Google, AI, or anything else.
